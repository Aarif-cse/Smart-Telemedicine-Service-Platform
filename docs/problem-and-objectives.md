# Problem and Objectives

## Problem Statement
Healthcare information (patient records, doctor details, appointments, prescriptions, consultation history) is growing rapidly, but many hospitals still manage it manually or with loosely structured records. This makes it slow to search for a patient or doctor, hard to track prescriptions and history accurately, and hard to capture the connections between patients, doctors, hospitals and appointments.

## Background
Hospitals are overcrowded, appointments take time, and specialists may not be available nearby. One patient may consult many doctors, and one doctor may treat many patients across departments. A system is needed that stores records and also manages these relationships efficiently. This project studies how such a system can be designed using tree-based and graph-based data structures.

## Problem Understanding
- **Existing problem:** Manual management of records and appointments is slow and makes quick medical support difficult.
- **Users affected:** Patients, doctors, hospitals, administrators, and people in remote or rural areas.
- **Information to manage:** Patient ID, doctor details, appointments, consultation records, prescriptions, medical history, doctor availability.
- **Functional requirements:** Register patients and doctors, search doctors quickly, book appointments, maintain medical records, manage online consultations.
- **Why DSA matters:** Balanced trees help search records efficiently; graphs represent relationships between patients, doctors, hospitals and appointments.

## Objectives
1. Understand the problem of delivering efficient healthcare online and the limits of manual appointment management.
2. Study Trees and Graphs as the foundation for the project.
3. Explore how tree-based structures can organize and search patient and doctor records.
4. Explore how graphs can represent patient-doctor-hospital relationships, and how heaps can prioritize emergency scheduling.
5. Prepare the conceptual foundation before implementation in later reviews.

## Stakeholders
- **Patients:** Search doctors, book appointments, attend consultations, manage prescriptions and records.
- **Doctors:** Manage appointments, consult remotely, issue digital prescriptions.
- **Hospitals / Clinics:** Manage patient information, doctor schedules and services.
- **Administrators:** Oversee the platform and maintain records.
- **General public:** Easier and faster access to healthcare.
